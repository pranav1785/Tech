The idea

Curiosity gets you in.
Consistency takes you further.

Guilds are designed to train and prepare strong competitors. You begin by exploring domains and learning through structured sessions and knowledge transfers, then move into regular practice, projects and competitions.

Your journey

Start curious.
Finish competition-ready.

You do not need to arrive as an expert. You need the willingness to learn, practise and keep showing up.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Meet the domains and find the problems that pull you in.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Build foundations through sessions, KTs and guided practice.

  3. 03

    Build

    Apply the craft to projects, prototypes and live problem statements.

  4. 04

    Compete

    Join focused teams and represent IIT Madras at the highest level.

  5. 05

    Lead

    Mentor the next cohort and move the shared knowledge forward.
